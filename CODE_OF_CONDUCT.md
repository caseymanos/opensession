# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and considerate.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person’s information without permission.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept moderation decisions and correction gracefully.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, and retaliation are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement will be proportionate, documented privately, and applied consistently.

For a confidential report, use the private contact path in [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md) and state that the report concerns community conduct. Do not include sensitive personal information that is not necessary to evaluate the report.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
